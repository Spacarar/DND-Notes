Ilighôn was one of two islands, along with [[Wavecrest]], that made up the [[Eyes of Silvanus]] in the [[Sea of Fallen Stars]]. It was situated close to the mouth of the [[Vilhon Reach]] and benefited from the protection of reefs as well as the magic of the Emerald Enclave. [2]

The island was accessible via a gap in the reef, known as the [[Farshore Straits]]. This passageway was guarded by the[[ Seven Sentinels of Silvanus]], a group of water and air [[Elementals]] who acted upon the direction of the enclave.
