A satyr druid from [[Northwest Faerûn (The North)]] Our home lies in a small unnamed wood between Cold Wood and Moon Wood. Our people worship the Green Goddess Eldath, but also have a close relationship with Auril and the bitter cold. Most people see Auril as evil, though our clan understands there can be safety in the cold and have learned from Auril's teachings. In the recent years, our shamans have began having issues with restoration magics and we have began to send out the few warriors we can spare to discover the cause for this fault in the weave. We know that Eldath and Auril will guide our hands to strike balance amidst the good and evil. The cycle of warming and cooling will never stop, such as the cycles of good and evil and we choose to find ourselves fighting to protect magic and nature above all else. I hope to return to my clan someday, but not ultimately I know the world is vast and there will always be imbalance. I seek to restore the balance in the places most harrowing.
