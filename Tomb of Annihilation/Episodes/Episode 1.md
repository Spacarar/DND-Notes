For the past several days in [[Waterdeep]], the talk of the streets and taverns has all been about the so-called death curse: a wasting disease afflicting everyone who’s ever been raised from the dead. Victims grow thinner and weaker each day, slowly but steadily sliding toward the death they once denied. When they finally succumb, they can’t be raised — and neither can anyone else, regardless of whether they’ve ever received that miracle in the past. Temples and scholars of divine magic are at a loss to explain a curse that has affected the entire region, and possibly the entire world.

we are ushered up by leads us up a room to a library-like room. we are met by an masked and unnamed figure [[Syndra Sylvane]]. with a dry raspy voice long baggy robes, and a silver mask. it is impossible to tell anything discernible about this figure though DM maybe mentioned it was a female. This figure claims to be dying and unable to fight back against the curse.

"My Harper sources have led me to a necromancer artifact [[The Soul Monger]] somewhere in [[Chult]]" There is discussion of how far away this place is or what lies waiting for us there. [[Syndra Sylvane]] claims to be able to teleport all of us to [[Chult]] together; much to the surprise of the group considering their condition. For the success of this mission [[Syndra Sylvane]] offers one item from the treasure room. Long drapes are pulled back, a giant steel door with a peep hole reveals an incredible amount of treasure behind it. [[Syndra Sylvane|Syndra]] says she will teleport us to [[Chult]] and stay with [[Wakonga O'Tamu]] in the only major civilization there.

As we are transported we immediately are hit with a wave of heat and humidity. [[Bryndor Aurilstag]] is not built for this at all coming from the cold north. it is kind of misty, the humidity is SO HIGH that foliage is literally growing out of stone. The workers around us are speaking an unfamiliar language filled with clicks and inhilations. The smell of spices and the must of the sea surround us. The city is colorful, full of murals depicting large reptiles (dinosaurs). [[Syndra Sylvane]] mentions [[The Thundering Lizard]], [[Kaya's House of Repose]]

[[Leet]] persuades a child, giving it a ribbon for being helpful and asks her who has the best wares in town and who gives the best deals. While she begins speaking with the children, one of them tries to pickpocket [[Leet]]. They finally answer that we should checkout [[The Red Bazaar]].

as we approach [[Wakonga O'Tamu]]'s house, we see 2 [[Aarakocra]] guards sporting a spearlike-weapon. they have shorter handles and a longer blade.

it is advised that we do not travel during rainy weather, and definitely be wary of the rivers during stormy weather. There are many types of dinosaurs in this area that roam the lands. we head towards [[The Red Bazaar]]. Huge slabs of meat are slung about, the streets and sewers are stained with blood. [[Leet]] insists on checking out a fish monger to snitch on the street kids who told her they steal fish from her.
