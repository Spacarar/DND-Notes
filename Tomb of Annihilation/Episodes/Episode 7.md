Last episode we flubbed a puzzle and had to battle some goblins and leveled up. I went for the circle of stars to try something. We found [[Inete]] tied up and freed her to ask her about the jug. She says she doesn't know much, but it is said that every stopper when opened will pour a different liquid. We should do more research regarding this jar.

I found 3 [[Batiri]] masks and the one from the chief. We take a long rest and begin traveling back down the river. We run into some guards and a priest who claim to be looking for a statue of a man carrying a crocodile, but I was able to deceive him and tell him we ran into some goblins in a hurry to leave from some rubble.

We take a long rest for the night and [[Leet]] takes first watch and sees 3 pairs of eyes looming about 10 feet high while we are resting.

We got ambushed by  2 regular crocodiles and [[Sehli Yarhibol]] made short work of them. We then ran across a fortress surrounded by brambles [[Camp Vegeance]]. The walls are large tall logs sharpened at the tip surrounded by a thick bramble bed. There are burned corpses strewn around the landing. It seems trouble is afoot. [[Leet]] sees the bodies were burned and appear to be previously undead. We healed like 7 soldiers.
