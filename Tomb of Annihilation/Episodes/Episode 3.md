We're rolling on the river with [[Inete]] on our canoe. An group of [[Aarakocra]] flew overhead and nearly shat on [[Sehli Yarhibol]]. [[Leet]] called out to them for being gross and they said they did not care for non-winged beings. [[Sehli Yarhibol]] argues that she is winged after all, and  they apologized. [[Leet]] managed to steal 5gp from them with mage hand before they flew away.

DM rolls for 2 encounters. I hope they're not bad. An arrow wizzes by my head and we see a group of 2 attacking the last of a few undead archers. They say they are part of [[The Emerald Enclave]] claiming to be trying to remove the undead from the valley. An organization of druids against unholy and unnatural things, such as the undead. They offered to let us buy things, but we didn't really have any cash.

We rested and then stumbled upon what first appeared to be a log, turned out to be a giant crocodile. It knocked half of us on our asses, but [[Inete]] did an awesome amount of healing and we were back on our feet. Just as the crocodile was going to flee with a final tail swipe, my spiny shield took out the last few remaining hp it had and it fell to the ground. [[Sehli Yarhibol]] started dismembering the carcass and fashioned a belt from it. We all took a much needed rest after the fight and called the session there.
