After an epic battle with a huge crocodile we begin our journey back on the river. The first day went off without a hitch. The second day we stumbled across 2 seahags (disguising themselves acting stranded with a broken canoe). [[Landric]] decided it would be a good idea to pull up alongside them, and [[Leet]] decided to play a short tune and asks if they know where [[The Soul Monger]] is. [[Leet]] doesn't realize that they are really sea hags. We fight them off and I finish off the last one by diving into the water horns first.
