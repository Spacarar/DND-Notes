
In the previous episode, we completed spelljammer as written [[Solara Sunfire]] destroyed the ring of shooting stars and sacrificed herself to destroy [[The Light of Xaryxis]]. We then headed over to the [[Planet of Malderon]] and were quickly accosted with gifts, among them was a longsword and shield, a warhammer, but also some questionable items. Notably a rubik's cube and a deck of cards. [[Morgana Noldra]] picks up and examines the deck of cards (later discovered to be [[Deck of many things]]. She saw magic so powerful it she was nearly blinded by it, yet decided to take it anyway. [[Morgana Noldra]] drew a card with a sun on it, she was told to think of a weapon and though "biological warfare". A horn appeared in her hand (like the instrument). We suspect it will summon biological warfare when used, though I'm quite scared to try it out. it sounds like it could end badly.

[[Astrid]] takes a look at the rubik's cube and a map comes out of it. [[Grimzod (Gargamel) Gargenhale]] says that is the location of [[Captain Ingerfell]]'s treasure. [[Morgana Noldra]] then finds out that she is allowed to draw 3 more cards for the rest of her life and she decided to draw all three of them at once. After the first draw and it's outcome, I definitely worry about what could possibly be next. First, she sees an exquisite spear appear in her hands. The second card tells her that she can have 2 more draws. The third card apparently has something to do with astrid?

[[Fel Ardra]] wanted to go back to [[The Rock of Bral]] and offered us a one way ride. We thought this could be a good way to sell all the loot we just got. We also considered the idea of buying [[Second Wind]] though [[Fel Ardra]] probably would be pretty pissed. 


We all went to bed, I went to be by myself and mull over how our adventure has gone and the strange findings we've made with the loot from [[Planet of Malderon|Malderon]]. [[Morgana Noldra]] was attacked in the middle of the night and so far no one was able to hear her. [[Bex Berghold]] was able to cast "Hold Person" on him. Towards the end of the battle, [[Morgana Noldra]] also summoned a lvl 4 drow fighter
